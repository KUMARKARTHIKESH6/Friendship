a letter, just for you

Dear Friend,

I don't say this often enough, so let me say it properly today: thank you for being exactly who you are. Somewhere between the inside jokes nobody else understands and the late-night calls about absolutely nothing, you became one of the steadiest, warmest parts of my life.

Thank you for the laughter that still makes my stomach hurt when I think about it later. Thank you for showing up — not just on the good days, but on the ones I didn't have the words to ask for help. Thank you for trusting me with the real you, and for never once making me feel like I had to be anyone other than myself around you.

You've taught me things no book ever could: how to forgive faster, how to celebrate someone else's wins like they're my own, how to sit in silence with a person and still feel completely understood. Every memory we've made, even the small, forgettable ones, has quietly built the friendship I now can't imagine my life without.

Here's to every memory still waiting to happen — the trips we haven't taken yet, the milestones we'll cheer each other through, the ordinary Tuesdays that somehow always turn into the best stories. Whatever life throws at us next, I hope we face it exactly the way we always have: together, and laughing.

Forever your friend,
Afrin Taj
